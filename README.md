📌 1. Objective
The primary goal of this project is to identify key factors influencing customer churn and support the telecom company in designing data-driven retention strategies.
Business Need


Customer churn increases acquisition and marketing costs.


Identifying churn drivers helps boost customer loyalty, profitability, and CLV.



📁 2. Dataset Overview


Total Customers: 7,043


Churned Customers: 1,869 (≈25%)


Average Tenure: 32 months


Average Monthly Charges: $64.76


Key Features: demographics, service type, subscription details, payment methods, contract types.



👥 3. Customer Demographics Insights


New customers show the highest churn rate (47%).


Long-term customers (>5 years) churn only 7%.


Senior citizens churn at ~27%, significantly more than younger groups.
Action: Focus retention efforts on new & senior customers.



🔌 4. Service Subscription Analysis


Fiber Optic users show the highest churn (38%).


DSL and non-internet subscribers churn less.


Bundled services reduce churn by increasing value offered.



🧾 5. Contract & Billing Insights


Month-to-month contracts: 45% churn


One-year contracts: 20% churn


Two-year contracts: 10% churn


Churn is highest among electronic check users.
Insight: Auto-pay and longer contracts improve retention.

Business Impact

📌 8. Key Recommendations


Offer discounts for yearly & two-year contract upgrades.


Promote credit card / auto-debit payment methods.


Provide loyalty rewards for customers in their first year.


Introduce bundled offers for fiber optic users.


Launch personalized communication for senior citizens.
